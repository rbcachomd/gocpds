Set up — change the access code

Type the code you want to use. Nothing is sent anywhere; this runs entirely in your browser. Then follow the two steps it shows you.

What this access control is, and what it is not. This system is a single file with no server. The access code separates roles and records who may do what — it is a workflow and accountability control. It is not a security control: it does not encrypt anything, and a person with technical knowledge can bypass it. Do not record it to your Quality Management Representative as a technical safeguard protecting personal health information. The real privacy protection here is different and stronger — no patient data is ever stored or transmitted by this application.

Independent verification is mandatory. These are arithmetic aids only. Every computed dose must be recalculated and countersigned by a second qualified person before compounding, in line with standard chemotherapy double-check practice. The Calvert formula was validated against isotopic GFR measurement; estimates derived from serum creatinine may overestimate renal function, particularly in low muscle mass, and are conventionally capped at 125 mL/min.
Dose computation is physician access only. Height, weight, and renal function above are recorded by nursing. Doses are prescribed and countersigned by the attending physician.

QR code for the patient’s phone

The QR code opens a mobile-friendly page of the general instructions for this regimen only. It deliberately carries no patient name, no chart number, and no personal data, so a lost printout cannot breach confidentiality.

6 · Governance Notice

This tool is a documentation aid, not a clinical decision support system. The embedded checklists reflect commonly used supportive-care practice and must be reviewed, edited, and formally approved by the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology and the Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee before institutional use. Every prescription remains the personal responsibility of the signing physician. Dose, frequency, and duration must be individualised for renal function, hepatic function, performance status, comorbidities, and drug availability in the institutional formulary.
